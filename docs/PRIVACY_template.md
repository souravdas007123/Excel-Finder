# Excel Finder: Privacy Policy

**Data controller:** <Your Company / Name>, <address>, <email>

## What stays on your computer (we never receive it)
Your Excel files, file names, folder paths, the search index, and every number you search for. All of this is processed only on your computer.

## What the license check sends to us
When you activate the license and about once a day while the app is online, the app sends:
- your **license key**,
- an **anonymous computer ID** (a one-way hash, not your name or serial number) and the **computer name**,
- the **app version**,
- two **counters**: how many searches and how many scans were run (numbers only).

We also see your **IP address** in our server logs when the request arrives.

## Why we need it
To check that your license is valid, to stop one license being used on more computers than you bought, to let you renew, and to give support.

## How long we keep it
While your license is active and for <12> months after it ends, then we delete or anonymise it. <Adjust.>

## Your rights
You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw consent, by writing to <email>. <Check what DPDP Act 2023 requires for your case.>

## Security
License communication uses HTTPS. The license server is protected by a strong password and regular backups.

## Changes
We will tell you in the app / by email if this policy changes in a way that matters.
